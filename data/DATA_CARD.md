# Data card — synthetic_restaurant_daily.csv
- **SYNTHETIC. Not real data.** Generated by `generate_synthetic_restaurant_data.py` (seed 42, 182 days, 8 items, 1,456 rows). Every row has `data_source=SYNTHETIC`.
- Columns: date, weekday, item, category, prepared_units, sold_units, wasted_units, stockout_units, unit_cost_nok, special_event, data_source.
- All assumptions (demand levels, weekday pattern, prep rule, costs) are invented; see the script docstring. Any "waste reduced by X" computed on it is a **demo on synthetic data**, never an impact claim.
- `recipes.csv`: SYNTHETIC dish → ingredient → grams per portion (invented, for the demo).
- `sample_plates/*.png`: drawn illustrations (not photos) from `make_sample_plates.py`, labelled on the image.
- No personal data. Regenerate: `python generate_synthetic_restaurant_data.py --seed 42`.
- On the day, the team may swap in real public data (cite it) or keep this, clearly labelled.
