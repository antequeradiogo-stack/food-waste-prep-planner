Impact · and how we'd measure it

What works today, and what we still have to prove

Prep suggestions

A working prototype, on synthetic data: portions per dish from the same weekday in recent weeks, plus a buffer, with the reason shown.

Plate-leftover estimates

Buckets of none / 25 / 50 / 75 / 100% × recipe grams, confirmed by a person before they're logged.

Stock tracking

A shelf camera counts packs, scales weigh the rest. Staff confirm before stock changes.

Order suggestions

Forecast × recipe grams minus stock, rounded to supplier packs. Exported for a person to place. The app never orders by itself.

People decide. The cameras look at food.

All data is synthetic

No real restaurant, guest or supplier data in the demo.

Humans approve everything

Every prep plan, order and waste entry. Nothing is ordered or thrown out automatically.

Food only, no faces

Cameras point at plates, bins and shelves. They estimate food; no faces are stored.

Honest limits

Without a scale, these are estimates, not exact weights. Forecasts need weeks of real data.
