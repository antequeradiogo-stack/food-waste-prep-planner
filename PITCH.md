# 3-minute pitch (hard stop) — structure mapped to judging
Organiser split: 30 s problem/user · 90 s prototype · 30 s benefit/evidence · 30 s limits/next step. Rehearse to **2:45**.

| Time | Section | Judging weight it serves | Say / show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | User & problem | Problem/user 20 + Impact 30 | One named role ("the head chef at a small restaurant at 10 am"), their decision, what goes wrong today. Diogo's restaurant experience = credibility; describe the workflow, no invented numbers. |
| 0:30–2:00 | Live demo | Prototype 20 + Responsible AI 20 | Load data (say "synthetic") → forecast → suggestion with reason → chef edits → **human approves** → assumptions & limits box. One happy path, no feature tour. |
| 2:00–2:30 | Benefit & how we'd measure it | Impact 30 | Beneficiary + plausible benefit + honest metric ("we'd compare waste logs 4 weeks before/after"). Label anything computed on synthetic data as a demo, not a result. A public source may frame scale (cite it on the slide). |
| 2:30–3:00 | Limits, safeguards, next step | Responsible AI 20 + Pitch 10 | 2 limits, 1 safeguard (human approval / no auto-action), 1 concrete next step (pilot with one kitchen using real logs). |

Two-stream design (one line): dining-room plate estimate = today's demo (photo → leftover bucket → staff confirm → grams per ingredient); kitchen bin = scale grams typed in by staff (demo); connected camera + smart scale = next step for measured waste.

Tie-breakers are impact then responsible AI → never cut those sections to fit time.
Avoid: brand-name dropping, "AI-powered" fluff, unsupported numbers, apologising for the demo.

## Slide outline (5 slides + backup)
1. **Title**: team name/number, challenge, one-line "for [user], who [need], we [what]".
2. **User & problem**: user, current workflow (3 steps), pain point. Assumptions clearly labelled.
3. **Demo** (live; screenshot on slide as backup).
4. **Impact & measurement**: beneficiary, benefit, metric, "demonstrated vs estimated" two-column split.
5. **Responsible AI & next step**: data sources, human oversight, limits, failure safeguards, next step.
- Backup slide: 3 screenshots of the demo flow + disclosure summary.
